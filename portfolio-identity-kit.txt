Portfolio Identity Kit: Modern Editorial Frame
1. Typography Selection
Heading Font: Playfair Display (Medium, 500 / SemiBold, 600) — Google Fonts

Rationale: A modern, editorial serif that introduces subtle sophistication and a high-end editorial feel without feeling traditional or dusty.

Body & UI Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Regular, 400 / Medium, 500) — Google Fonts

Rationale: An ultra-clean, modern geometric sans-serif that balances the serif headings and ensures code, metrics, and case studies read effortlessly.

2. Color Palette (Hex Codes)
Background (Near-White): #F8FAFC (Slate Porcelain — crisp, airy, and modern)

Text (Near-Black): #0F172A (Deep Obsidian — 15.8:1 contrast ratio)

Card & Border Surface: #E2E8F0 (Soft Slate)

Accent Color (Derived from Favicon Orb): #3B82A6 (Calm Cerulean — peaceful, refined, non-aggressive accent)

3. Logo & Favicon Assets
Logo: Minimalist white line-art hat silhouette set against dark surfaces or crisp light backgrounds.

Favicon: Soft 3D sky-blue to deep teal gradient orb (#BCE7F0 ➔ #3B82A6).

4. Two-Line System Style Note (For AI Workspace)
Typography & Palette: Headings: Playfair Display (500/600), Body: Plus Jakarta Sans (400/500). Background: #F8FAFC, Primary Text: #0F172A, Borders: #E2E8F0, Accent: #3B82A6.

Mood & Layout: A modern, calm, editorial aesthetic with subtle luxury framing—generous whitespace, understated slate borders, and crisp serif typography that elevates case study evidence without stealing the show.
